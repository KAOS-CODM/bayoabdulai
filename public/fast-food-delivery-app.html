01 Project Overview

The goal of this project was to design a user-friendly, fast and trustworthy food delivery app that simplifies the ordering process while providing a delightful experience from browsing to delivery.

Problem / Challenge

  • Users struggle to find their favorite food quickly.
  • Complicated checkout process leads to cart abandonment.
  • Lack of real-time order tracking and transparency.
  • Limited personalization and relevant recommendations.

02 Research

I used both qualitative and quantitative methods to understand users, their needs and frustrations.

Methods

  • User Interviews
  • Online Surveys
  • Competitor Analysis

Participants

  • 15 Participants
  • Age 18–45
  • Frequent food delivery users

03 Key Insights

  • Users want a faster way to find and reorder meals.
  • Real-time tracking and clear delivery details build trust.
  • Personalized recommendations improve engagement.
  • Multiple payment options increase convenience.

04 User Pain Points

  • Hard to discover food or restaurants.
  • Too many steps to complete an order.
  • Unclear delivery time and order status.
  • Limited offers that match user preferences.

05 Design Goals

  • Make food discovery quick and effortless.
  • Simplify the ordering and checkout process.
  • Provide real-time order tracking.
  • Offer personalized experiences and relevant offers.
  • Build trust and reliability in the platform.
