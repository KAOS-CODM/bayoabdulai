01 Project Overview

The goal of this project was to design a personalized career growth platform that uses AI to match users with relevant opportunities, recommend skills to learn, and track their progress over time.

Problem / Challenge

  • Users lack personalized guidance for career growth.
  • Hard to track skills progress and job application status.
  • Overwhelming information with no clear direction.
  • No integrated platform for learning, practice and job search.

02 Research

I used both qualitative and quantitative methods to understand users, their needs and frustrations.

Methods

  • User Interviews
  • Online Surveys
  • Competitor Analysis

Participants

  • 15 Participants
  • Age 18–35
  • Students & Professionals

03 Key Insights

  • Users want clear direction on what to learn next.
  • Personalized job matches increase engagement.
  • Progress tracking motivates users to stay consistent.
  • AI-powered feedback and tips are highly valued.

04 User Pain Points

  • Don't know which skills to focus on.
  • Can't find relevant job opportunities.
  • Hard to prepare for interviews.
  • No way to measure career growth.

05 Design Goals

  • Provide AI-powered career recommendations.
  • Track skills, learning and application progress.
  • Simplify complex data with intuitive dashboards.
  • Create a personalized and engaging user experience.
